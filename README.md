# Hi there 👋

## Come along on my journey of merging my data skills into a detour on my career.

PhD in Petroleum Engineering, with a research background in reservoir characterization and digital rock physics. After my postdoc, I moved into data analytics, working mainly in Tableau and picking up SQL along the way.

When ML and AI started becoming the technology everyone was talking about, I got curious about applying it back to petroleum engineering. So I took an Applied Data Science certificate program to build a real foundation in machine learning and AI.

Now I'm combining both: applying ML/AI to petroleum engineering, with a focus on reservoir characterization and improving oil recovery.

This repo is where I'm documenting that work: real projects, built from the ground up, at the intersection of petroleum engineering and ML/AI.

## What I'm Working On 🎯

- Applying machine learning to oil recovery, across both conventional and unconventional reservoirs.
- Explaining the petroleum engineering fundamentals behind each project clearly enough for people outside the field.
- Sharing the process, not just the finished results.

## Skills & Tools 🛠️

  **Petroleum Engineering:** Reservoir characterization, digital rock physics, Petrel, CMG, Petra, Techlog, GOHFER
  **Data Analytics:** Tableau, Power BI, SQL
  **ML/AI:** Python, Flask, Machine Learning, Neural Networks
